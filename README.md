# Favorite Friends

<!-- drh:show -->
Click the star at the top center of a friend's town leaderboard portrait, between the trophy and ascension badges.

- Gold stars mark favorites. Click again to remove a favorite and turn the star gray.
- Favorites move to the front of their online or offline group. Friends with the same favorite status are ordered by trophies. Your own slot stays first.
- Favorites persist across restarts in the mod's local `favorites.json` file. Star friends again to recreate favorites from the old Flash mod.
<!-- /drh:show -->

Install the ZIP from the Mods page in DRH Launcher, then enable Favorite Friends. Requires Dungeon Rampage Haxe V17 and DRH Mod API 1.2.
